PokeData is a modern pokedex for fans: look up Pokémon, regions, and details in a fast, easy-to-use app. Whether you are filling a living dex, planning a team, or just browsing for fun, this pokedex keeps the information you need close at hand.

WHY CHOOSE POKEDATA?

PokeData is built for everyday use. Open the app, search a name, and get types, stats, evolution, and flavor in a clean layout. Sign in to sync favorites across devices, or explore as a guest when you only want a quick lookup. Offline browsing keeps recently viewed Pokémon available when you have no signal.

This pokedex is made for Pokémon fans who want a reference that feels current: clear type icons, readable text, and navigation that stays out of the way. No cluttered menus. No keyword stuffing. Just a pokedex you can actually use.

KEY FEATURES

• Complete pokedex browsing
Scroll a vast list of Pokémon from every generation. Each row shows the National Dex number, name, types, and sprite so you can scan quickly.

• Fast search
Type a name in the search bar to jump straight to the Pokémon you have in mind. Useful when you only remember part of a name.

• Filters by type and generation
Narrow the list by type, generation, and other filters so you can answer questions like “which Water Pokémon are in this generation?” without leaving the pokedex.

• Regions
Open a region to see which Pokémon belong there. Handy when you are playing a specific game or comparing Pokédex entries across regions.

• Detailed profiles
Open any Pokémon for height, weight, abilities, base stats, weaknesses, evolution chain, and game text. The detail screen is the heart of this pokedex.

• Favorites
Save the Pokémon you care about. Favorites are there for team ideas, shiny hunting targets, or your personal living dex. An account is required to persist favorites.

• Guest mode
Explore the pokedex without creating an account. Sign in later if you want favorites and synced settings.

• Offline-friendly cache
The app stores data locally so browsing stays smooth and you can still open Pokémon you have already loaded when you are offline.

• Language
Switch the app language between Portuguese and English from Account. Game text from the PokéAPI follows the language you choose whenever a translation is available.

HOW TO USE THIS POKEDEX

1. Open the Pokédex tab and scroll or search.
2. Use type and generation filters when you are exploring, not hunting a single name.
3. Open a Pokémon to read stats, weaknesses, and evolution.
4. Visit Regions when you want a map of the Pokédex by place.
5. Sign in and tap the heart to keep favorites.

WHO IT IS FOR

Casual players who need a pokedex during a playthrough. Competitive fans who want a quick stat and type check. Collectors tracking favorites. Anyone who prefers a focused Pokémon reference over a social feed.
